## The problem

Public speaking feedback usually depends on someone watching you and remembering what they saw. We wanted a tool that takes a recording of a talk and gives specific, timestamped feedback on how the speaker looked and sounded.

## How it works

The video goes through three analyses in parallel. YOLOv8 reads facial emotion, MediaPipe Pose tracks posture, gaze and gestures, and Whisper produces a timestamped transcript. GPT-4.1-nano combines these into structured feedback scored across six dimensions. Flask and React, connected with Socket.IO, show progress live while the video is processed.

There's also an audio-only mode, which uses GPT-4o-mini to analyse speech quality and suggest rewrites of the transcript, and AWS Polly to read them back.
